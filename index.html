The method behind this plan

1. One shared trackerLaunches go wrong on forgotten tasks and on information that never reached the team. One file, open to everyone, with an owner on every line.
2. Approvals and budget firstApproval delays and late budget questions slow a launch more than anything else. Who approves, how fast and how much are agreed in the first week.
3. Produce earlyProduction is the step teams leave for the last week. Here it starts four weeks before launch and ends ten days before.
4. Reread, follow up, remindEvery asset is read twice, every step has a status, and reminders do the remembering.
A generic method, free to use. Your statuses stay in your browser.
