# Launch Planner

Try it: **[rechidwahiba-cell.github.io/launch-planner](https://rechidwahiba-cell.github.io/launch-planner/)**

Pick a launch date and a few options, and get a dated launch plan in four phases: strategy, production, launch week and after the launch.

- Three different plans: digital product, service, or programme and event
- Adapts to business or consumer buyers, organic or paid launches, with optional steps for quotes and invoices
- A status on every step: To do, In progress, Ordered, Validated, Done
- "Essentials only" view with the dozen steps that matter most
- "My version": change dates and wording, add or remove steps
- Export to Excel (with a status list and colours) or to a calendar file, share a link or send the plan by email

## The method behind it

1. **One shared tracker.** Launches go wrong on forgotten tasks and on information that never reached the team.
2. **Approvals and budget first.** Who approves, how fast and how much are agreed in the first week.
3. **Produce early.** Production starts four weeks before launch and ends ten days before.
4. **Reread, follow up, remind.** Every asset is read twice, every step has a status.

## How it works

A single HTML file with no framework, no build step and no server. The plan comes from a list of steps with offsets in days from the launch date, filtered by the options chosen. The Excel file is generated in the browser. Statuses and edits are saved in the browser only: nothing is sent anywhere.
