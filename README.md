# Launch Planner

Pick a launch date and what you are launching, and get a dated plan: what to do before, during launch week and after.

- Works for a digital product, a service or a programme
- Adapts to business or consumer buyers, and to organic or paid launches
- Tick steps off as you go: progress is saved in your browser only
- Copy the plan as text or print it

## How it works

A single HTML file with no framework and no build step. The plan comes from a fixed list of steps with offsets in days from the launch date, filtered by the options you choose. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## Why I built it

Most launches follow the same pattern: decide who it is for, fix the message and the offer, build and test, announce, then review against one number. This tool turns that pattern into dates.
